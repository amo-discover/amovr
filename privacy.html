Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026. Applies to the Amo VR app for VR headsets.

In short

Amo VR has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not run any server for the app and does not receive, collect, store, sell or share any information about you or about what you watch.

What stays on your headset

To work, the app keeps these items in its own private storage on your headset only:

  • the links, playlists, files and media server addresses you added or opened recently;
  • where you stopped in each video, so it can continue from there;
  • your settings: language, environment, screen size and position, picture format, audio, subtitle and quality choices, volume;
  • cookies and site data of the media servers and web apps you open in the app, kept like a web browser keeps them.

You can delete any recent item in the app, and everything at once by clearing the app's storage in the headset settings or by uninstalling the app. The app has backups turned off, so this data is not copied to any cloud backup.

Network connections

Amo VR connects only to the addresses you enter or open: video links, playlists, media servers and web apps. Like any player or browser, it then sends those services the usual technical information of an internet request, such as your IP address, the app's user agent and the requested address. Those services are run by their owners and their own privacy policies apply. The app makes no other network requests: no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or update checks to the developer.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to play the links, playlists and servers you choose.
  • Read videos and audio (storage on older systems): only when you open Files, to list and play the videos and playlists on your headset or USB drive. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Keep awake: so the headset's Wi-Fi does not sleep during playback.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the age set by your country's law).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about privacy, write to the developer email shown on the Amo VR page of the store where you got the app.
